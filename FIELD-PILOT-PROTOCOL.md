# FloodBridge field pilot protocol (draft)

## Pilot question
Can a low-bandwidth evidence register improve the timeliness and traceability of crossing-condition information and make dependencies of essential services more visible to local response teams?

## Site selection
Select a small, agreed set of crossings or river gauges with the relevant county authority and local community. Do not install equipment on unsafe structures or active flood channels without engineering and safety approval. Establish site ownership, access permissions and emergency procedures first.

## Baseline
- Map the location and asset dependencies with local responders.
- Agree the meaning of each passability category and how it will be communicated.
- Identify official sources for rainfall, river stage and disaster warnings.
- Survey gauge datum and local thresholds with qualified hydrologists / engineers.
- Define how long each type of observation remains operationally fresh.

## Field observation protocol
- Observe from a safe location only; no crossing attempts and no wading.
- Record site, coordinates, observation time, source category and visible evidence.
- Record whether depth/stage is a measured value or unavailable.
- Do not convert uncertain visual estimates into exact centimetres.
- Capture contradictory reports; do not delete them to create consensus.
- Apply a safety check before re-sharing any user-generated statement.

## Sensor comparison
If a water-level node is deployed:
- Compare at least daily (or at an agreed frequency) against a physical staff gauge under safe conditions.
- Record sensor mounting height, local datum, measurement range and calibration.
- Include invalid / no-echo and communications outages in results.
- Test rain, fog, splash, floating debris, condensation, power loss and network outage.
- Do not treat distance-to-water as a road depth or river discharge measurement.

## Evaluation metrics
- Time from observation to first record.
- Fraction of reports with valid coordinates, timestamps and source metadata.
- Age distribution of records displayed as current.
- Agreement between duplicate observations and reference gauge readings.
- Frequency and handling of conflicting reports.
- Number of essential-service dependencies documented and confirmed by local responders.
- User comprehension: do people distinguish “no report” from “verified clear”?
- False reassurance incidents, privacy complaints and unsafe data-collection attempts.

## Stop conditions
Stop deployment if the app encourages hazardous verification, stale data appear live, unapproved thresholds trigger public warnings, sensor datum is uncertain, or personal data are collected without governance approval.
